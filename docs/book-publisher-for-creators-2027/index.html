Common mistakes

Four mistakes that hold a creator book back

Paying to be published

A company that charges for printing, editing or marketing earns from you, not from readers. That is a vanity press.

A book “for everyone”

A book with a clearly defined readership sells more easily. Your topic is hiding in your community's questions.

Wasting the pre-order phase

Without pre-orders, sales spread out over time and the chance of reaching a bestseller list drops.

Online only, launch only

Without bookstores and without ads after the launch, a book quickly loses visibility.

Questions

Book publisher for creators: your questions answered

How many followers do I need for a book deal?

There is no fixed number. What counts is an active community that trusts you and a topic it wants more of from you.

Do I have to write the book myself?

No. Your writing mentor turns your content, notes and voice messages into the manuscript, in your own voice. Your name is on the cover.

Do I need a literary agent?

Not for a specialized publisher. Large trade houses mostly work through agents, who typically take 15% of domestic earnings. With an audience, going directly is often faster.

Where will my book be sold?

On all major platforms and in bookstores, online and offline. Distribution is worldwide, so your book can be ordered in bookstores around the world.
